Before you subscribe

Can’t find the answer? Message us on WhatsApp. Replies within 48 working hours (Sunday to Thursday, 12 pm to 9 pm).

All questionsPolicies
When do I hear back after subscribing?

Within 48 working hours. Working days are Sunday to Thursday, 12 pm to 9 pm, and contact is on WhatsApp.

How does follow-up work?

You send the weekly review on your review day, and I reply with recorded adjustments (video or voice). On the Basic plan the review is every two weeks. Daily contact is on WhatsApp, with replies within 48 hours.

Is it suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. The program is built on your current level, every exercise is explained, and you can send clips of your form for technique correction.

I train at home. What equipment do I need?

The questionnaire has a field for the equipment you have (dumbbells, resistance bands, barbell…) and your plan is built around it.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer after filling in the questionnaire. As soon as you submit it, your order number, the amount, and the account details appear. Transfer the amount, then upload a photo of the receipt from your order page. Your subscription is confirmed once the payment is verified.

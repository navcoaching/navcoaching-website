1. From keywords.md, take the top 5 commercial keywords.
2. For each, form the AI query: "best <keyword> <current year>".
3. Send ChatSEO, word for word:
   "Who ranks top 10 on <best keyword year>? Which of those pages are
   rankings or listicles, and do they mention my site?"
4. Build geo.md: query, the listicles ranking, whether we are in them,
   the site behind each one, and a contact to reach.
5. Suggest title edits adding "best" and the year to our matching pages.
   Run them through page-brief and ship-page. Never edit directly.
6. Draft one short, personal outreach email per listicle we are missing
   from. I send them myself.
